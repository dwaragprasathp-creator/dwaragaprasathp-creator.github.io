Selected engineering work

Projects & systems.

A selection of ongoing and completed work across robotics, embedded engineering, intelligent monitoring and geospatial applications.

01 / FEATURED PROJECTIn development

AURA One — Autonomous Mobile Robot

A differential-drive mobile robotics platform combining ESP32-based hardware with a ROS 2 Jazzy and Gazebo simulation workflow. The development roadmap includes sensor integration, wheel-odometry feedback, IMU-based orientation, obstacle detection, mapping and navigation. A planned extension explores autonomous sapling monitoring along a defined route, with environmental and soil sensing.

ROS 2 JazzyGazebo HarmonicESP32Embedded C++SensorsNav2 / SLAM
02Research & development

Multimodal Motor Health Monitoring

A condition-monitoring workflow investigating motor faults through vibration and acoustic signals. Work includes signal conditioning, filtering, windowing, FFT-based analysis, feature extraction and machine-learning classification, with a multimodal score combining complementary signal information.

PythonDSPFFTMachine LearningSensor Analytics
03Research

Urban Heat Analysis — Hyderabad

A geospatial research study using Landsat 8/9 imagery, thermal bands, spectral indices, SRTM terrain data and ERA5 climate variables to investigate urban heat patterns across multiple years. The workflow explores machine-learning models, spatial validation, temporal testing and explainable analysis.

Remote SensingGISLandsatPythonXAI
04Platform concept

NearStock / Fyndly — Local Product Discovery

A local-commerce platform concept designed to help people discover product availability in nearby stores. The proposed system connects local inventory discovery with a digital interface, with a Flutter frontend and an Express/SQL backend explored during development.

FlutterExpress.jsSQLProduct Discovery

Microcontroller-based experiments and prototypes involving sensor acquisition, actuator control, wireless connectivity and automation logic. Practical work includes ESP32 boards, ultrasonic sensing, servo actuation and camera-module integration.
